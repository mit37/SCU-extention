About the Likeness Score

A 0–100% estimate of how much students tend to like a professor, blended from their RateMyProfessors rating (55%), "would take again" percentage (25%), inverted difficulty (10%), and signals pulled from crowdsourced syllabi (10%), such as lenient late/attendance policies vs. strict no-exceptions ones. Confidence is lower when a professor has few ratings or no syllabi on file.
